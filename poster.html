CLIMATE · ANTALYA · COP31

COP31 Collapses: No Deal in Antalya

After two days of overtime, the longest climate summit on record ends without an agreement — the EU, Britain, Panama and island states reject a draft that never mentions fossil fuels. President Murat Kurum concedes: “There is no agreement on this text.”

NORTH AMERICA · ELECTIONS

Democrats Clinch 258 House Seats, Biggest Since 1992

Chuck Hubbard’s win over Virginia Foxx in North Carolina gives Democrats their 258th seat on Sunday — a gain of 44, the largest House majority either party has won in three decades, with the Senate also theirs.

EUROPE · BELGIUM

Belgium Grounds Itself as the Nation Strikes

Air-traffic controllers join Monday’s national strike, halting almost all commercial flights from 07:00 to 21:00 — 429 flights and 59,500 passengers at Brussels Airport alone — as unions protest the €10bn austerity budget.
